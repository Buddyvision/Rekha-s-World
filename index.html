Welcome to the world of Rekha

A Celebration of Grace & Elegance

There are few people in this world whose very presence brings an immediate sense of calm, warmth, and deep reassurance. For me, that person is my mother, Rekha Rani Kalita Das.

Born on the 17th of September, 1977, she has spent her life not only guiding others through the complexities of the human mind as a psychologist and shaping young minds as a teacher, but anchoring our family with unmatched grace, patience, and boundless love.

A truly sweet and beautiful soul inside and out, her kindness leaves a lasting mark on everyone fortunate enough to know her. She is the quiet strength behind our hardest days and the brightest light in our happiest moments. Rekha World is our way of honoring that extraordinary heart—a celebration of a life dedicated to healing, understanding, and unconditional love.
